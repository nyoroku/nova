CRESCENT ISLAND CLARITY

Understanding Crescent Island Sanctuary Fees

Crescent Island is a private wildlife sanctuary where you walk among giraffes, zebras, and wildebeests without predators. Nova provides the boat transfer across Lake Naivasha (KES 6,500 / $65 USD per boat for up to 7 guests) and captain standby. Sanctuary walking entry fees are payable directly at the island entrance gate (Citizen Adult KES 800, Resident Adult KES 1,100, Non-Resident $33 USD).
