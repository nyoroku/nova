Driving Directions from Naivasha Town

  1. From Naivasha Town roundabout or Nairobi highway exit, turn onto Moi South Lake Road heading towards Hell's Gate National Park.
  2. Proceed past Buffalo Mall and continue along the tarmac for approximately 14 km.
  3. Look for the clear Nova Boat Rides Base signage on the lakeside (right-hand side when coming from town).
  4. Security clearance at the gate is immediate for reserved Nova guests. Quote your booking name or captain reference.
