A visit to Crescent Island Game Sanctuary consists of two separate costs: (1) Nova's boat transit and standby fee, and (2) the Sanctuary's conservation entrance fee paid directly at their gate.

What Is Always Included

Every ride includes your KMA-licensed captain guide, fitted life jackets for all adults and children, boat fuel, and complimentary mineral water.

What Is Excluded

Island sanctuary entrance fees (Crescent Island), meals, personal travel insurance, and optional tips for your captain.

Wildlife Sightings Reality

Our captains know Lake Naivasha's hippo pods and fish eagle roosts intimately. However, wildlife is wild, so sightings vary by weather, water level, and time of day.
