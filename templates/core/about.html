ABOUT NOVA

The Lake-Access Layer for Naivasha

Nova is not merely another Lake Naivasha boat operator. Nova is the lake-access layer for visitors and hotel partners: discover a ride, start from the most convenient approved jetty or launch point, and coordinate the complete stay and lake day through one trusted local team.

Our Philosophy

Fewer handoffs. Better lake days.

Visitors to Lake Naivasha frequently face fragmented logistics: booking accommodation with one hotel, searching for an unverified boat ride on a public beach, negotiating prices without transparency, and struggling to coordinate gate clearances or water level access.

Nova coordinates the entire experience: your boat experience, your hotel pickup or jetty access, your Crescent Island guide, and your accommodation options under one clear standard.

The Nova Standards

  • ✓ Verified Operations: We only claim partner access points where physical depth and agreements are verified.
  • ✓ Mandatory Marine Safety: Every passenger is fitted with an approved life jacket before stepping onto any jetty.
  • ✓ Truth in Pricing: Tiered prices published publicly with no hidden tourist markups at the launch site.
