Quick Package Quote

Unique Island Sanctuary

Cross the lake. Continue on foot.

Crescent Island is a volcanic crater rim rising out of Lake Naivasha. Because no predators inhabit the sanctuary, you can walk among wild giraffe, Burchell's zebra, wildebeest, and gazelle with complete peace of mind.

Nova coordinates your private boat transit directly to the island's western jetty, pairs you with a sanctuary guide, and waits to cruise you back across hippo lagoons.
